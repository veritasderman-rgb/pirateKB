> EN: My contribution will deal with the future of European regulation and transparency of social media platforms based on insufficiency of the self-regulatory approaches applied by the tech giants themselves (e.g. in case of Code of Practice against Disinformation). The intervention will focus primarily on the European Democracy Action Plan (EUDAP) but partially also DSA, which represent a new opportunity for the future of European regulation, if rightly approached, and promoting the concept of digital sovereignty and standard-setting on the world stage.
