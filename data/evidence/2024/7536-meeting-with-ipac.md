# Meeting with IPAC

Schůzka 26. 6. 2024. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2024-10-30 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was contacted with a meeting request by Mrs. Iva Merheim-Eyre, Regional Liaison for Europe, Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China (IPAC). Mrs. Merheim-Eyre wanted to present me the work of IPAC, a not-for-profit organisation aimed at resistance to Chinese foreign influences and protection of human rights in China.
