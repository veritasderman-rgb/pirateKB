# Meeting with Taiwanese Production to new documentary INVISIBLE NATION

Schůzka 3. 12. 2024. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2024-12-13 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Ms. Amy Shepherd and Mr. Yik Kei Leung from the Think-Film Impact Production (Taiwan) asked me for a meeting in the EP. They wanted to present me their new documentary "INVISIBLE NATION", about Taiwan’s first female President, Tsai Ing-wen. They also wanted to talk about possible ways we could collaborate and potentially bring the film to Brussels.
