# Meeting with H.E. Bálint Ódor, Permanent Representative of Hungary

Schůzka 23. 9. 2024. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2024-12-13 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked on behalf of Coreper II trilogue coordinator Mr. Balázs Széchy for a meeting with H.E. Bálint Ódor, Permanent Representative of Hungary to the EU. The aim of the meeting was to discuss the legislative work for the upcoming Hungarian Council Presidency, with focus on the procedural rules concerning the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
