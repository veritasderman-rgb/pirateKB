# Meeting with Commissioner McGrath

Schůzka 11. 3. 2026. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-03-24 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of the of the Cabinet of Michael McGrath, European Commissioner for Democracy, Justice, the Rule of Law and Consumer Protection, I was ask to hold a meeting on the topic of the Digital Omnibus – data protection/GDPR aspects – for which I´m Shadow Rapporteur in the LIBE committee. The meeting with the Commissioner took place in the EP in Strasbourg and my Advisor J. Obry was present as well.
