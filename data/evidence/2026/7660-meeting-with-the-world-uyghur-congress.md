# Meeting with The World Uyghur Congress

Schůzka 15. 9. 2026. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-10-27 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

The World Uyghur Congress representatives were undertaking an advocacy mission to Strasbourg in March during the plenary session, and asked me for a meeting to discuss issues related to EU-China relations, transnational repression, and the possibility of co-organizing an event on Uyghur rights at the European Parliament, with which I agreed.
