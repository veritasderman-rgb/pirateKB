# Meeting with Vincent Strubel

Schůzka 14. 4. 2026. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-05-04 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of the Counsellors in charge of relations with EP at the French Permanent Representation I was asked for a meeting with them and with the Director General and other representatives of the French Cybersecurity Agency (ANSSI). They wanted to discuss on the cybersecurity topics, and more particularly on CSA2, NIS2 and Digital omnibus - legislative files I´m responsible for as Rapporteur or Shadow Rapporteur. I accepted the meeting request and we met in the EP in Brussels. I was accompanied by my Advisors Mr. Diegel and Mr. Obry.
