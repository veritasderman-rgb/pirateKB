# Meeting with the European Data Protection Supervisor

Schůzka 24. 2. 2026. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-03-24 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Mr. Wojciech Wiewiórowski (European Data Protection Supervisor) contacted me with a meeting request. I accepted the meeting request and we met in EP in Brussels together with my Advisor Mr. Obry. The aim of the meeting was to discuss the most important challenges related to the protection of personal data and the use of artificial intelligence systems by European Union institutions, bodies, and agencies.
