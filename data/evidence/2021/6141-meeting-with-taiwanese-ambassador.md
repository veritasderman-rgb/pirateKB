# Meeting with Taiwanese Ambassador

Schůzka 3. 12. 2021. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

After our EP delegation's visit to Taiwan, the Ambassador Mr. Tsai was very interested to learn about my thoughts and feedback from the meetings in Taipei. Mr. Tsai was very grateful for our support to Taiwan and he also wanted to discuss potential future collaborations.
