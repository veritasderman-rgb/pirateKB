# Call with Salome Samadashvili, Badri Japaridze and Grigol Gegelia

Schůzka 24. 9. 2021. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a Zoom call with Ms. Samadashvili, Mr. Japaridze and Mr. Gegelia regarding the situation in Georgia before the upcoming local elections in Georgia. It was an excahnge of views before my arrival to Georgia as part of EP official election observation mission.
