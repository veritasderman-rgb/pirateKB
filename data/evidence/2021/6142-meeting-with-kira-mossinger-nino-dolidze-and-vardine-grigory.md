# Meeting with Kira Mossinger, Nino Dolidze and Vardine Grigoryan

Schůzka 9. 12. 2021. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Ms. Dolidze and Ms. Grigoryan (representatives of ISFED and HCAV - independent domestic citizen election observation organizations in Georgia and Armenia and member organizations of the EPDE - a network of 15 independent European citizen election observation organizations) asked me and my assistant Ms. Koubková for an online meeting. The aim of the meeting was to discuss the recently held elections and political developments in Georgia and Armenia.
