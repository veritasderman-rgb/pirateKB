# Meeting with Mr. Gegelia, Mr. Khazaradze and Mr. Japaridze

Schůzka 20. 12. 2021. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked by Mr. Grigol Gegelia to hold an online meeting with him and with Mr. Mamuka Khazaradze and Mr. Badri Japaridze (Founders of Georgia’s TBC Bank). The aim of the meeting was to discuss the urgent developments in the criminal case against Mr. Khazaradze and Mr. Japaridze in Georgia, which might be politically motivated.
