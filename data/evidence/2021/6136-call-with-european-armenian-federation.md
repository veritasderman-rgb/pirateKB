# Call with European Armenian Federation

Schůzka 14. 10. 2021. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked by Mr. Kaspar Karampetian, president of the European Armenian Federation for Justice and Democracy (EAFJD), and Ms. Heghine Evinyan, executive director of the EAFJD, for a brief meeting to discuss the current developments in Armenia and in the region. We met online, together with my assistant Ms. Koubková.
