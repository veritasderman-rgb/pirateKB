# Meeting with Bundesverband deutscher Banken e.V. (Association of German Banks) (online)

Schůzka 17. 1. 2021. Zapsal/a: David Wagner (zveřejněno 2021-02-02 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Identification number in the EU transparency register: 0764199368-97

The most important issue of the meeting was ensuring consistency and clear single regulation for all the stakeholders even after implementing effects of The Directive (https://oeil.secure.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/popups/ficheprocedure.do?reference=2020/0268(COD) , where they fear that some implementations might lead to two different standards on being applied to one product at once (mostly in field of insurance). We are going to check up on that directly with DG Fisma.
