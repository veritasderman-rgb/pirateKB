Schůzka 2. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2019-11-06 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I joined for a lunch with other Members of the European Parliament’s Internal Market and Consumer Protection Committee. An informal discussion touched on the topics around upcoming activities of the Committee and priorities of the Finnish presidency in the Council.
