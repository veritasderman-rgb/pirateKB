# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Mr. Grigol Gegelia

Schůzka 20. 11. 2019. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-01-04 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting with Mr. Gegelia to discuss topics like the state of democracy, sustainability and the green perspective on possible EU-membership of Georgia. We discussed also future strategies and possible cooperation with MEPs and Greens/EFA group.
