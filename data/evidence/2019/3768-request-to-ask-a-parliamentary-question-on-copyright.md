# request to ask a parliamentary question on copyright

Schůzka 4. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2019-11-05 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was contacted by Diego Naranjo from European Digital Rights in order to ask a parliamentary question regarding the implementation of article 17(7) of the Directive on Copyright in the Digital Single Market. I have agreed to ask the question and subsequently did so. The question is available at the Parliament's website:
