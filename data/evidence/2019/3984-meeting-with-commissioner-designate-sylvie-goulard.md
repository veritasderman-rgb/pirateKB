# Meeting with Commissioner-designate Sylvie Goulard

Schůzka 18. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2020-01-07 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

The meeting was an opportunity to hear the Commissioner-designate on her European vision, her willingness to increase collaboration and support large majorities in and between EU institutions, and to exchange our views on digitization. It was also an opportunity to let her elaborate on her possible future collaboration with EVP Margrethe Vestager. I have shared my conception of cybersecurity and we also touched on her receiving money from a US think tank and why she had to return money to the Parliament spent on employing her APAs.
