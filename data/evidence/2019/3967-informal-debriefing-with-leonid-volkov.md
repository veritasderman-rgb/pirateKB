# Informal debriefing with Leonid Volkov

Schůzka 13. 11. 2019. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-01-04 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I attended a debriefing from Leonid Volkov who is one of the most interesting and thoughtful Russian politicians. We discussed update on the current state of political situation in Russia, the prospects for Russian opposition and the role of new media for mobilization of citizens in Russia.
