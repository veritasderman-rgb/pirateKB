# Meeting with MEP Daniel Freund for the anti-corruption intergroup

Schůzka 5. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Michaela Hnizdilova (zveřejněno 2021-03-30 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Daniel Freund and Marcel Kolaja discussed possibilities to set up the anti-corruption intergroup, its financing and a possibility to have a Pirate in the board. Given a lot of duties Marcel already has as Vice-President of the European Parliament and the IMCO coordinator for his political group, he recommended Mikuláš Peksa to be a board member. Marcel expressed his intention to become a member of the intergroup.
