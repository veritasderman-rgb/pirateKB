# discussion with a representative of Transparency International

Schůzka 3. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2019-11-06 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I ran into a representative of Transparency International at a presentation of OLAF 2018 report. We discussed the conflict of interest of Andrej Babiš, as well as his Čapí hnízdo prosecution. We also talked about transparency of the European Parliament and the European institutions, as well as I shortly presented my plan on transparency and digitization in my capacity as Vice-President of the European Parliament.
