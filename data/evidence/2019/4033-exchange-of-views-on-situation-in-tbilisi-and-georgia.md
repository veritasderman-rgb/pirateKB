# Exchange of views on situation in Tbilisi and Georgia

Schůzka 4. 12. 2019. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-01-24 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I organized a roundtable event for other MEPs and their assistants with my Georgian guests - mainly activists from NGOs and watchdog organizations. They described current situation and protests in Tbilisi, we briefly touched the topic of Georgia's borders also.
