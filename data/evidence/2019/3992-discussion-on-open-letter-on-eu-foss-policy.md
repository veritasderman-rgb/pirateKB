# discussion on Open Letter on EU FOSS Policy

Schůzka 26. 9. 2019. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2020-01-10 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I participated in a debate on an open letter providing Policy Recommendations on Free and Open Source Software. I shared my views on the institutional support for the FOSS, its role in the much-discussed concept of European digital sovereignty, and ways to increase the cooperation of policy-makers with FOSS communities
