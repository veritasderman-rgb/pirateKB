# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Mr. Jan Šnaidauf (Minister Tomáš Petříček's assistant)

Schůzka 6. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting with Honza Šnaidauf in Prague. We discussed what I do in my committees and what is my focus. Then we dived into discussion about 5G and current affairs and Eastern partnership relationships and summit. We promised to stay in touch about overlapping issues, as I am one of only two Czech MEPs in AFET and SEDE committees. I invited him for a lemonade.
