# Call with Kaspersky lab

Schůzka 2. 6. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-08-11 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a call with Jochen Michels, representative of the Kaspersky lab - a multinational cybersecurity and anti-virus provider. The topic of our conversation was ethical hacking and stalkerware. We had questions related to their cooperation with state actors, though we haven't learnt anything in this regard.
