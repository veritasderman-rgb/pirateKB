# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Mrs. Lucia Arguellova (Člověk v tísni)

Schůzka 5. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in my office in Brussels with Mrs. Lucia Arguellova from the NGO Člověk v tísni. We discussed ČvT activities in mostly South America region and Pirates' stance towards what is going on in Brazil or Chile and how can we cooperate there. I provided contacts to MEPs from Greens/EFA group who focus on this topics more, as I don't have much capacity to cover it.
