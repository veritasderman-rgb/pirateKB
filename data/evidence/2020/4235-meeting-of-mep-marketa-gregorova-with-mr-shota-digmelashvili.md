# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Mr. Shota Digmelashvili (The Shame Movement)

Schůzka 17. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in Brussels with Shota, a representative of The Shame Movement. We discussed civic society and activism in Georgia and its potential to influence the situation there. I also asked about their aspirations to go into politics, however they see themselves more as a stepping stone between citizens and parties and a glue between opposition parties.
