# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Georgian Dream representatives

Schůzka 12. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in the MEPs bar in Strasbourg with Georgian Dream (government) representatives before the inter-parliamentary PAC meeting. They provided me previously promised materials regarding the political prisoners cases and documents about the violent dispersion of protests. They gave me a USB disc with a footage from some of the demonstrations, however I refused, as I don't want to plug unknown USB discs into my computer. They promised to send it to me via cloud or other solution.
