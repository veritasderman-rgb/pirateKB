# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with the Azerbaijani Ambassador H.E. Mr. Fuad Isgandarov

Schůzka 3. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in my office in Brussels with Mr. Fuad Isgandarov, Ambassador of the Republic of Azerbaijan to the Kingdom of Belgium and Grand Duchy of Luxembourg and the Head of Mission to the European Union. It has been a mutually introductory meeting. We discussed the Eastern Partnership, role of Azerbaijan in the EaP and comming report in AFET and INTA regarding to EaP. I have been given a traditional Azerbaijani tea in a wooden box.
