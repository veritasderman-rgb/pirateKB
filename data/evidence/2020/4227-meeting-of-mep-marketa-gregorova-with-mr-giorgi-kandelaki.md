# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Mr. Giorgi Kandelaki

Schůzka 4. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

We met in the European Parliament with Mr. Kandelaki from European Georgia Party, together with my colleague from the Greens group, Mrs. von Cramon and our advisors. It was an introductory meeting, as we knew a lot about the situation in Georgia already, we asked a few questions specifying what is going on.
