# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with the Ambassador Tomáš Szuonyog

Schůzka 27. 1. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in my office i Brussels with the Ambassador Tomáš Szuonyog, Permanent Representative to the Political and Security Committee (COPS), Deputy Head of the Czech Permanent Representation to the EU. We discussed on what topics I focus and how it correlates with the Czech foreign affairs policy. We touched in more detail the future Czech Council presidency and preparations for that.
