# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with Georgian civil society experts

Schůzka 21. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I joined a meeting in the EP organized by Open Society European Policy Institute (OSEPI) - the policy office of the Open Society Foundations in Brussels. They brought a group of civil society representatives from Georgia to Brussels to share their insights on the implications of the alarming recent setbacks in the area of democracy and rule of law, as well as their analysis on the state of implementation of the EU - Georgia Association Agreement.
