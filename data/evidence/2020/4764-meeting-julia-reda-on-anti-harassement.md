# meeting Julia Reda on anti-harassement

Schůzka 30. 6. 2020. Zapsal/a: Marcel Kolaja (zveřejněno 2020-10-19 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Although the relevant internal body dealing with harassment cases had stated harassment behavior from Julia's APA, the administration of the European Parliament did not allow her to dismiss the APA's contract on harassment grounds. Julia Reda briefed me on her case and told me that her main goal is that we can avoid such situation from happening in the future.
