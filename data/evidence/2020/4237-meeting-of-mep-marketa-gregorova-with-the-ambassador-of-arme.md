# Meeting of MEP Markéta Gregorová with the Ambassador of Armenia Mr. Tatoul Markarian

Schůzka 21. 2. 2020. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2020-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a meeting in my office in Brussels with H.E. Mr. Tatoul Markarian, the ambassador of Armenia in Brussels. We discussed the situation in Armenia with regard to agreements with the EU, and naturally also the situation around Nagorno Karabach and relationship with Azerbaijan. I mostly listened, I shared my opinion and explained what are my concerns just on the Trio Strategy, which the ambassador supported. I was given a traditional Armenian wine.
