# Meeting with Members of the Georgian Parliament

Schůzka 13. 9. 2022. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-01-06 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a personal meeting in the EP premises in Strasbourg with Georgian delegation - namely with Ms. Maka Botchorishvili (Chair of the European Integration Committee of the Parliament of Georgia), Mr. Giorgi Khelashvili (First Deputy Chair of the Foreign Relations Committee, Georgia) and Ms. Tamar Diogidze (II Secretary, Mission of Georgia to the EU). We discussed the current situation in Georgia and in the region in light of the Russian agression on Ukraine, as well as the state of play of Georgia´s EU membership application and fulfillment of the 12 points before Georgia can be granted a candidate status.
