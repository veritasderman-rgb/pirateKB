# Datenna - export control meeting

Schůzka 26. 1. 2022. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked, as an INTA committee member and after having participated to the Export Control Forum hosted by the Commission last month, by representatives of Datenna to have an online meeting. Datenna is a Dutch company which develops software tools for economic intelligence on China exclusively used by governments worldwide. They wanted to share with me their new research - an analysis of China’s defence and military-civil industries and a radar on European investments in China.
