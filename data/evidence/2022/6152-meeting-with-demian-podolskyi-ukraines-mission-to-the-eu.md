# Meeting with Demian Podolskyi (Ukraine’s Mission to the EU)

Schůzka 11. 2. 2022. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2022-03-17 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

H. E. Mr. Vsevolod CHENTSOV, Ambassador, Head of Ukraine’s Mission to the EU, kindly requested an appointment between His Excellency and me, in order to discuss the latest developments in Ukraine. Due to overlapping commitments in our agendas, the meeting was held between Mr. Demian Podolskyi (Counsellor, Mission of Ukraine to the European Union) and my policy advisor Mrs. Koubek, who attended the meeting on my behalf.
