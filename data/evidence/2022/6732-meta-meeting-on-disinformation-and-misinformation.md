# Meta - Meeting on disinformation and misinformation

Schůzka 26. 9. 2022. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-01-06 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

As a member of INGE Committee, I was asked by Mr. Simone Gobello for a meeting with him and other representatives of Meta. The aim of the meeting was the presentation of how Meta operates across their platforms to protect them from foreign interference and what do they do on disinformation (and misinformation). I asked about their failures to hear out whistleblowers, such as Sophie Zhang, and their involvement in radicalisation or violence, such as in Myanmar.
