# Lunch meeting with Taiwan's ambassador Chen

Schůzka 19. 10. 2022. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-01-06 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a lunch meeting in Strasbourg with the new Ambassador of Taiwan, Mr. Remus Li-Kuo Chen Chen, who has very recently taken up his post in Brussels. It was an introductory and courtesy meeting. We exchanged contacts for further communication on the Chips Act.
