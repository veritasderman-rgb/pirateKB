# Support democracy &amp; Stop executions in Iran

Schůzka 27. 3. 2023. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-04-28 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I had a video-call with representatives of Alliance For Public Awareness, a coummunity of representatives from specific minorities established to make the public aware of issues of importance for peace, security and the promotion of human rights. The topic of the online meeting was a discussion about the human rights situation in Iran, particularly regarding the imprisonment and the executions after the nationwide protests, the sanctions against Iran, and the recent chain poisoning of schoolgirls. I was asked to spread awareness about the situation in Iran and also about their open letter. I promised to do so.
