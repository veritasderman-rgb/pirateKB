# Meeting with Georgian delegation

Schůzka 15. 3. 2023. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-04-28 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I joined a meeting in the EP in Strasbourg with delegation of Georgian opposition leaders from Strategy Builder, UNM, Droa and Girchi More Freedom. The Georgian opposition representatives wanted to share with MEPs the information regarding the recent developments in Georgia, provide their views and plans regarding further steps on how to achieve the fulfilment of 12 recommendations, how to overcome obstacles created by GD on the way to EU integration, and ways of coordination among pro-Western opposition parties.
