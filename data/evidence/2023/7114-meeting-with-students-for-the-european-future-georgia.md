# Meeting with "Students for the European Future", Georgia

Schůzka 26. 4. 2023. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-11-22 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Students from non-governmental organization called "Students for the European Future" from Georgia contacted me with a request for a meeting. We met in person in my office in EP in Brussels. The students introduced to me their action plan, a concrete project to strengthen the European integration process of Georgia and increase civic engagement. They also wanted to obtain my recommendations and visions for the European future of Georgia.
