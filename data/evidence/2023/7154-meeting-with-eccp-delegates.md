# Meeting with ECCP delegates

Schůzka 5. 12. 2023. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2024-01-08 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Representatives of the European Coordination of Committees and Associations for Palestine (ECCP) in Brussels requested a meeting in order to discuss the situation in Palestine, in particular in the Gaza Strip. They wanted do discuss the possible steps that the EU and international community can take to address this critical situation. I accepted the meeting, together with my assistant, and we met in person in the EP premises in Brussels. I was informed by the representatives about the development in Gaza and we discussed possible solutions.
