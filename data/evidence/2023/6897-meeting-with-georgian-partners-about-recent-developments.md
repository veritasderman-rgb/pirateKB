# Meeting with Georgian partners about recent developments

Schůzka 29. 3. 2023. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2023-04-28 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked on behalf of International Partnership for Human Rights (IPHR) to meet with several partners from leading Georgian civil society organisations who were visiting Brussels. They wanted to share with me the first-hand updates on the recent developments in Georgia relating to respect for human rights and the rule of law and the related impact on Georgia's European integration, particularly by relation to the EU's 12 recommendations. The meeting took place in the EP in Brussels.
