# Meeting with Slavomír Maňásek

Schůzka 9. 2. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-03-03 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of the co-presidents of Volt Europa, Francesca Romana D’Antuono and Mels Klabbers, Mr. Slavomír Maňásek asked me for a meeting in person in Brussels, where he was representing the co-presidents and the European board at the Volt Belgium General Assembly. He wanted to discuss primarily my experience with cooperation with the Volt delegation in the European Parliament, but also the current political situation in Czechia and the prospects of progressive politics in Czechia in the upcoming years.
