# Meeting with World Uyghur Congress delegation

Schůzka 11. 2. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-03-03 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of Ms. Zumretay Arkin, Vice President of the World Uyghur Congress, I was asked for a meeting with World Uyghur Congress delegation, which came to Strasbourg to meet with various MEPs during the plenary week to discuss the upcoming priorities regarding the Uyghur question. I accepted the invitation and met the delegation in the EP premises in Strasbourg, together with my assistant Paul Diegel. I was introduced to the new leadership and listened to their current activities.
