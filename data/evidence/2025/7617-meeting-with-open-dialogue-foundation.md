# Meeting with Open Dialogue Foundation

Schůzka 4. 11. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-01-19 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked for a meeting on behalf of the Open Dialogue Foundation, whose delegation was visiting Brussels. I accepted the meeting request and we met with Mr. Abdulehet Nur, Mr. Salih Hudayar, Dr. Mamtimin Ala and Ms. Turkan Mukhlissova in my office in the EP in Brussels. The aim of the meeting was to provide first-hand testimonials about China’s threats to digital and financial integrity and targeted persecution of individuals based on their online activity.
