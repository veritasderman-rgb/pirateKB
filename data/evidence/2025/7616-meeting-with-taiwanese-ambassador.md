# Meeting with Taiwanese Ambassador

Schůzka 14. 10. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-01-19 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of the Parliamentary Liaison Division of the Taipei Representative Office in the EU and Belgium I was asked for a meeting with the new Ambassador, Prof. Dr. SHIEH, Jhy-Wey, who served as Ambassador to Germany in the past 9 years. We met in the EP in Bussels and it was an introductory, courtesy meeting.
