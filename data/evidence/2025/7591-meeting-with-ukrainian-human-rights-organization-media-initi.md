# Meeting with Ukrainian human rights organization Media Initiative for Human Rights (MIHR)

Schůzka 12. 6. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-06-29 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was asked by Ms. Anna Zevako, on behalf of the Ukrainian human rights organization Media Initiative for Human Rights (MIHR), for an online meeting. MIHR is focusing on documenting the unlawful detention of civilians in the Russian-occupied territories and advocating for their release. Therefore, they wanted to discuss the broader issue of civilian detentions and potential avenues for advocacy. They contacted me due to my letter to one of the Ukrainian political prisoners, Serheii Kovalskyi. They asked me to also send my answer through them, as they are in contact with his lawyer, and medialize his case. I already had that in plan before this meeting.
