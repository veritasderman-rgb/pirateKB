# Meeting with Daitove - Georgian grassroots civic movement

Schůzka 26. 3. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-06-29 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was contacted with a meeting request on behalf of the Georgian grassroots civic movement "Daitove". I met with several key leaders in the premises of the EP in Brussels. The aim of the meeting was to discuss the ongoing protests in Georgia, as Daitove has played a central role in coordinating the protests, countering Russian and Georgian Dream propaganda, and monitoring the elections.
