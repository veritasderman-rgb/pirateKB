# Meeting with REPowerEU initiative

Schůzka 25. 9. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-01-19 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

A coalition of civil society organisations — including Razom We Stand, B4Ukraine, the Institute for Energy Economics &amp; Financial Analysis (IEEFA), the International Partnership for Human Rights (IPHR), the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air (CREA), Urgewald and Climate Action Network Europe (CAN-E) — visited Brussels for an advocacy tour focused on the Regulation on phasing out Russian gas and the REPowerEU initiative. I was asked to hold a meeting with them, on behalf of Mr. Ivan Hortal Sanchez (Razom We Stand). As a Shadow Rapporteur for the Regulation on phasing out Russian gas imports, I accepted the meeting request in order to listen to their point of view.
