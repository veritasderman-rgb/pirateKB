# Meeting with Hungarian Ambassador

Schůzka 1. 12. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2026-01-19 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

On behalf of Deputy Permanent Representative of Hungary to the European Union, Ms. Katalin Molnár, I was asked for a meeting concerning the draft REPowerEU regulation, as was called the regulation to phase out Russian gas by the Council. As I´m the Shadow rapporteur for this file, I accepted the meeting request and we met in EP in Brussels to discuss the matter. I listened to the Hungarian concerns, but made my position in this case is clear - that we need to end the imports of Russian gas as soon as possible.
