# Online call from the Georgian diaspora

Schůzka 21. 3. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-06-29 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I was contacted with a meeting request on behalf of the civil platform "Generations for Georgia", which unites many Georgian emigrants from all countries. I had a short online call with the representatives and we discussed the crisis situation in Georgia, the continuous protests of the Georgian society and also the visions of the Georgian society on the ways to peacefully exit the current crisis.
