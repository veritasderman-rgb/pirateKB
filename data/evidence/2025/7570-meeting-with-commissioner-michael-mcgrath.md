# Meeting with Commissioner Michael McGrath

Schůzka 12. 2. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-03-03 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Mr. Michael McGrath, Commissioner for Democracy, Justice, the Rule of Law and Consumer Protection, proposed me an exchange of views during the plenary session in Strasbourg in February, on the GDPR file “General Data Protection Regulation: additional procedural rules relating to the enforcement of the Regulation”. As Rapporteur for this file, I accepted the meeting invitation. The Commissioner wanted to hear about my views and the way forward.
