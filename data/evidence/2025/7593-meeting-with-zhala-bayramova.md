# Meeting with Zhala Bayramova

Schůzka 18. 6. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-06-29 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

I met in the EP in Strasbourg with Ms. Zhala Bayramova, Azerbaijani Human Rights Lawyer and daughter of Dr. Gubad Ibadoghlu (Azerbaijani academic and anti-corruption fighter prosecuted by president Aliyev´s regime and nominated for the Sakharov Prize for Freedom of Thought 2024). I met again with Zhala and had a follow-up discussion with her about the health situation of her father and campaigning activities for his support and how can I assist her. I was also informed about the continuous deliveries of Russian gas and oil through Azerbaijan to Europe and plan to raise it in one of my committees or delegations.
