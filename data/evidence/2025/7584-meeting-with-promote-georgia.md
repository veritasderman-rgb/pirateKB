# Meeting with Promote Georgia

Schůzka 19. 3. 2025. Zapsal/a: Nadia Barcalova (zveřejněno 2025-06-29 v Evidenci kontaktů a schůzek).

Ms. Gugulashvili and Ms. Pitalskaya from the Georgian NGO "Promote Georgia" asked me for a meeting. I accepted and met them in the EP in Brussels, together with my trainee Ms. Antoniuk. The aim of the meeting was to discuss the worsening crisis in Georgia, including the situation of several unlawfully imprisoned Georgian political prisoners arrested during the protests against the increasingly repressive Georgian regime.
