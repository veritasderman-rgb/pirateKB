# Prioritní otázka k písemnému zodpovězení P-000506/2023: Digital Services Act – transparency obligation

Prioritní otázka k písemnému zodpovězení P-000506/2023 (P-9-2023-000506), podáno 16. 2. 2023. Autor: Marcel Kolaja. Adresát: Evropská komise. Otázka europoslance je jeho vyjádření, ne stanovisko strany; odpověď je odpověď orgánu EU.

## Otázka

*Text v jazyce: angličtina (český překlad EP nezveřejnil).*

Subject: Digital Services Act – transparency obligation

Does the Commission believe that the transparency reporting obligations to which very large online platforms are subject under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act) are sufficient to enable restrictions on the freedom and pluralism of the media to be identified and, pursuant to Article 35 of that regulation, effectively mitigated?

Submitted: 16.2.2023

## Odpověď

Odpověď (orgán EU) ze dne 16. 3. 2023: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/P-9-2023-000506-ASW_CS.html

*Text v jazyce: angličtina.*

Answer given by Mr Breton

on behalf of the European Commission

(16.3.2023)

Safeguarding fundamental rights, including media freedom and pluralism, is at the core of the Digital Services Act (‘DSA’)[1].

The DSA imposes due diligence obligations in relation to content moderation, tailored to categories of providers of intermediary services. It provides safeguards against unjustified removal of legal content, including media content, and possibilities of redress for content providers. The DSA also sets out rules on the application of the terms and conditions of those service providers, including having due regard to media freedom and pluralism online. Providers designated as very large online platforms (‘VLOPs’) and very large online search engines (‘VLOSEs’), shall also manage and mitigate systemic risks stemming from their service, including in relation to freedom and pluralism of the media[2].

These obligations are accompanied by transparency requirements, enhancing accountability of providers of intermediary services. In particular, VLOPs and VLOSEs shall report to the Commission and their Digital Service Coordinator, and make publicly available information on their risk management.

[1] Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 October 2022 on a Single Market For Digital Services and amending Directive 2000/31/EC (Digital Services Act), OJ L 277, 27.10.2022, p. 1–102.

[2] Article 11 of the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union, OJ C 326, 26.10.2012, p. 391–407.
