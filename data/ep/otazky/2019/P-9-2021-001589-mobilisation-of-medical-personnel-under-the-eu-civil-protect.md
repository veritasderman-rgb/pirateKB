# Prioritní otázka k písemnému zodpovězení P-001589/2021: Mobilisation of medical personnel under the EU Civil Protection Mechanism – Czechia’s eligibility

Prioritní otázka k písemnému zodpovězení P-001589/2021 (P-9-2021-001589), podáno 22. 3. 2021. Autor: Marcel Kolaja. Adresát: Evropská komise. Otázka europoslance je jeho vyjádření, ne stanovisko strany; odpověď je odpověď orgánu EU.

## Otázka

*Text v jazyce: angličtina (český překlad EP nezveřejnil).*

Subject: Mobilisation of medical personnel under the EU Civil Protection Mechanism – Czechia’s eligibility

Several Member States, including Czechia, have been struggling with a severe lack of medical staff to treat patients with COVID-19.

Neighbouring Slovakia recently requested help from the EU Civil Protection Mechanism. In response, Denmark offered to send three doctors and five nurses, and Belgium offered two nurses, a doctor and a team leader.

1. What requirements do Member States have to meet to be eligible for such assistance under the EU Civil Mechanism Protection and does Czechia fulfil these criteria?

2. Have the Czech authorities requested medical help under the EU Civil Protection Mechanism?

Supporters[1]

[1] This question is supported by Members other than the author: Mikuláš Peksa (Verts/ALE), Markéta Gregorová (Verts/ALE)

## Odpověď

Odpověď (orgán EU) ze dne 29. 4. 2021: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/P-9-2021-001589-ASW_CS.html

*Text v jazyce: angličtina.*

Answer given by Mr Lenarčič

on behalf of the European Commission

(29.4.2021)

When an emergency overwhelms national response capacity, any country in the world can make a request for assistance under the Union Civil Protection Mechanism (UCPM)[1]. This requires an official request from a government to the European Commission’s Emergency Response Coordination Centre (ERCC)[2], outlining the precise needs.

Czechia has sought support through the UCPM twice in response to COVID-19, but those requests did not cover emergency medical teams. In October 2020, it requested 150 ventilators and 1,000 high-flow nasal oxygen devices. The request for ventilators was met through a combination of offers from the Commission’s rescEU stockpile of critical medical equipment hosted by Romania, and through offers from Austria[3] and the Netherlands[4]. On 5 March 2021, Czechia issued a new request for 15,000 COVID-19 vaccines, which was fulfilled by France.

Czechia is also eligible to apply for financial support under the Emergency Support Instrument (ESI). The Mobility Package within ESI includes three strands, one of which covers the transfer of medical personnel within the EU and from third countries into the EU. The EU can finance up to 100% of the eligible costs of transport and operational costs under this strand. So far, the Commission has not received any application from Czechia for this strand of the ESI Mobility Package.

[2] https://ec.europa.eu/echo/what/civil-protection/emergency-response-coordination-centre-ercc_en

[3] 15 ventilators.

[4] 105 ventilators.
