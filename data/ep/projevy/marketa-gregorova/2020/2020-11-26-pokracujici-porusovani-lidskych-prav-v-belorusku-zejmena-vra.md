Projev europoslance je jeho vyjádření, ne stanovisko strany. Text je v jazyce originálu; každé vystoupení má odkaz na záznam na europarl.europa.eu.

## 2020-11-26 12:17 – Pokračující porušování lidských práv v Bělorusku, zejména vražda Romana Bondarenka

*Markéta Gregorová (Verts/ALE), za politickou skupinu, originál: angličtina* · stenozáznam: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-9-2020-11-26-INT-4-131-0000_EN.html

on behalf of the Verts/ALE Group. – Mr President, ‘I am going out’. These were the last words of Raman Bandarenka before he was kidnapped and beaten to death by men in plain clothes. His words were sent to a group chat on Telegram, the encrypted messaging app which is shielding millions of Belarusian citizens from the government’s internet censorship. This tragedy, one on an already long list, epitomises the current state of things in Belarus. An illegitimate government keeps denying its own people fundamental human rights by way of illegal detentions, torture and even murder.

It should be evident by now that the sanctions issued, until this moment, have failed to produce results. The regime did not take even a single step back so we must take a step forward. It is time to introduce further restrictive measures, direct them more at Lukashenko himself, and ban the export of any European goods and technology that are helping this regime, such as drones and spyware.
