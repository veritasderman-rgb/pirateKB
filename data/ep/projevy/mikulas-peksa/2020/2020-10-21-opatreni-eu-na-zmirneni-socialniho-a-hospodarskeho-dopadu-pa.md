## 2020-10-21 15:58 – Opatření EU na zmírnění sociálního a hospodářského dopadu pandemie COVID-19 (rozprava)

*Mikuláš Peksa (Verts/ALE), originál: angličtina* · stenozáznam: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-9-2020-10-21-INT-3-152-0000_EN.html

Madam President, the pandemic has proven two things. Most importantly, it showed us how much we are interconnected and depend on each other. If we suffer or prosper, we are in it together. However, it also showed us how disorganised and weak we are in the face of new challenges. Member States closing borders without coordination, applying different travel conditions, providing contradictory information, and competing for supplies among themselves look like complete chaos, a dangerous chaos with human lives at stake.

We need a coordinated European response. We need stronger rule of law conditionalities, and we need to move the health competencies from national to central EU level, with the backing of a stronger European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control. Only by acting together will we overcome this pandemic.
