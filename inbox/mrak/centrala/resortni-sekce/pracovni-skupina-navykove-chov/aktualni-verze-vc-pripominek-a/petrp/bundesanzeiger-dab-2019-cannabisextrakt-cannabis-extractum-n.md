Annex
Standardized Cannabis Extract
Cannabis extractum normatum
Definition
Standardized extract produced from whole or crushed, flowering, dried shoot tips of the female
plants of Cannabis sativa L. (Cannabaceae).
Content:
- Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabinol (C21H30O2; Mr 314.5): minimum 1% and not more than 25% (m/m) for
the extract and 90 to 110 percent of the nominal content indicated on the label
- Cannabidiol (C21H30O2; Mr 314.5): not more than 10% (m/m) for the extract and 90 to 110 percent
of the nominal content indicated on the label
Production
The extract is produced by a suitable extraction procedure, such as heptane-, ethanol- or CO2-
extraction. The obtained crude extract is, where applicable, refined, dissolved in a suitable fatty oil,
such as medium-chain triglycerides, grape seed oil or similar, and adjusted to the indicated content.
The cannabinoid acids are decarboxylized at a suitable step during production.
Characteristics
Appearance: yellow to brown liquid.
The extracts dissolved in a suitable fatty oil have characteristic Relative densities and Refractive
indices.
Test for identification
The test is carried out by high-performance thin-layer chromatography (2.8.25).
Test solution: (0.5 mg/ml THC or CBD). The amount weighed of the preparation must be adjusted
taking into account the indicated main cannabinoids. The corresponding amount weighed is filled up
to 10.0 ml with a suitable solvent (e.g. 2-propanol R or methanol R). The solution is subsequently
filtered through a membrane filter with a nominal pore size of 0.45 µm. This solution is used as the
test solution.
Reference solution a: 5 mg cannabidiol RN and 5 mg Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol RN are dissolved in
10.0 ml methanol R.
Reference solution b: 2.5 ml reference solution a is diluted to 10.0 ml with methanol R (Intensity
marker IM).
Reference solution c: 5 mg cannabinol RN and 5 mg cannabidiol RN are dissolved in 10.0 ml methanol
R.
Intensity marker:  Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol.
Stationary phase: TLC-plates with octadecylsilyl silica gel F254 R (2 to 10 µm).

Test for purity
Cannabinol: Not more than 1.0 percent. The test is carried out by liquid chromatography (2.2.29)
using reference solution III as described in section ‘Assay’.
The percentage of cannabinol (C21H26O2) is calculated by the following formula:
Content = Fu · er · Gr · d/Fr · eu ·100 in mg · ml-1 C21H26O2.
Fu = Peak area of cannabinol in the chromatogram obtained with the test solution.
er = Weight of cannabinol in milligrams.
Gr = Content of cannabinol in percent.
Fr = Peak area of cannabinol in the chromatogram obtained with reference solution III.
eu = Weight of the preparation in grams.
d = relative density in g · cm-3.
Water (2.5.12): Not more than 0.5 percent, determined on 0.200 g substance.

Assay
The test is carried out by liquid chromatography (2.2.29).
Test solution: (0.2 mg/ml THC or CBD) The weight of the preparation must be adjusted taking into
account the main cannabinoids. The corresponding amount weighed is filled up to 25.0 ml with
ethanol 96% R. After filtration through a regenerated cellulose membrane filter with a nominal pore
size of 0.20 µm, the solution is used as the test solution.
Reference solution I: 5.0 mg Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol RN is dissolved to 25.0 ml with methanol R. The
calibration solution has a concentration of 0.200 mg · ml-1.
Reference solution II: 5.0 mg cannabidiol RN is dissolved to 25.0 ml with methanol R. The calibration
solution has a concentration of 0.200 mg · ml-1.
Reference solution III: 5.0 mg cannabinol RN is dissolved to 25.0 ml with methanol R (stock solution).
With this solution, a calibration solution with a concentration of 0.002 mg · ml-1 is generated through
dilution with methanol R.
Reference solution IV: 5.0 mg Δ8-tetrahydrocannabinol RN is dissolved to 25.0 ml with methanol R.
1.0 ml of this solution is mixed with 1.0 ml of reference solution I and filled up to 10.0 ml with
methanol R.
The chromatography can be performed as follows:

Test conditions
Detection: Spectrometer at 225 nm
Application system: Sample loop
Injection volume: 10 µl; test solution, reference solution
Recording time: 20 min
Relative retention (relative to Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol, tR approximately 8.7 min)
- Δ8-tetrahydrocannabinol:
approximately 1.04
- Cannabidiol:
approximately 0.58
- Cannabinol:
approximately 0.83
Suitability test
Resolution: At least 1.5 between the peaks of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol and Δ8-tetrahydrocannabinol
in the chromatogram obtained with reference solution IV.
Precision: The reference solutions I and II are injected 6 times and the areas of the peaks
corresponding to Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol and cannabidiol are determined.
The test can only be evaluated, when the relative standard deviation of the individual values from
the mean value is not more than 1.0 percent.
Evaluation
A. The percentage of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol (C21H30O2) is calculated by the following formula:
Content = Fu-a · er-a · Gr-a · d/Fr-a · eu ·100 in mg · ml-1 C21H30O2.
Fu-a = Peak area of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol in the chromatogram obtained with the test solution.
er-a = Weight of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol in milligrams.
Gr-a = Content of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol in percent.
Fr-a = Peak area of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol in the chromatogram obtained with reference solution I.
eu = Weight of the preparation in grams.
d = relative density in g · cm-3.
B. The percentage of cannabidiol (C21H30O2) is calculated by the following formula:
Content = Fu-b · er-b · Gr-b · d/Fr-b · eu ·100 in mg · ml-1 C21H30O2.
Fu-b = Peak area of cannabidiol in the chromatogram obtained with the test solution.
er-b = Weight of cannabidiol in milligrams.
Gr-b = Content of cannabidiol in percent.
Fr-b = Peak area of cannabidiol in the chromatogram obtained with reference solution II.

Storage conditions
Tightly sealed, protected from light, stored at 2 to 8 °C.
Labeling:
The calculated percentage of Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinol and cannabidiol should be stated on the
container. The type and quantity of any stabilizers should be stated.
