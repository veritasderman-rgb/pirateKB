Problems in relation to Cannabis extracts
●Extraction technologies
●
Different extraction technologies result in extracts, with different properties.
●
Unclear which extraction technology is the best for what application.
●
Legal limitation for the use of certain extraction technologies.
●Impurities
●
Impurities are also concentrated during the extraction.
●
Residues of the extraction solvent may remain.
●
No existing quality standards.
●Legal barriers
●
The production of extracts may be conflicting with legal barriers because THC is concentrated.
●
Special audits are required for the production of consumables and medical products – GMP / GLP
●
Natural products and natural compounds have no or comparatively weak protection of IP Rights.

●Development of medical products
●
Generally complicated and very expensive.
●
Even more complex because of the entourage effect.
●
Has to compete with existing drugs – potentially not interesting for Big-Pharma.
Problems in relation to Cannabis extracts
●Entourage effect
●
Combination effect of different substances
●
Compounds with no own activity can influence positive and or negative effects
●
Very complex to understand and reproduce
●Clinical research
●
Clinical research needs a lot of knowledge and effort to be correctly designed.
●
Extremely expensive and takes long time.
●
Clinical studies have to be granted by the state.
●
Has to compete with existing "gold standard" treatments

Conclusion
●Problems
●
The biggest problem is the black market which results in cross-selling of other drugs and complete
loss of state control, besides creating damage due to not necessary criminal cases.
●
Cannabis businesses are facing a lot of problems because of the dynamics of the whole industry.
●
The state has currently no ways to take real benefit of a functioning cannabis industry.
●
Developments of medical products are very expensive and stand in competition with Big-Pharma.
●Solutions
●
Change the laws in order to regulate the industry instead of pushing it to the black market.
●
If the state would create a real benefit of the cannabis industry, the security for businesses would increase.
●
With increasing regulation and protection Cannabis business could develop a lot better.
●
The Cannabis allows the creation of new industry models which benefit the state instead of shareholders.
●
The cooperation of the state with commercially active cannabis companies can lead to success in short time.
