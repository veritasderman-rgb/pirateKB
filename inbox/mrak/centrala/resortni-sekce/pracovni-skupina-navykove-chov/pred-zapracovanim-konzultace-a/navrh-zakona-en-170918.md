THE TEXT OF THE PART OF THE ACT ON ADDICTIVE SUBSTANCES,  AS AMENDED, WITH THE PROPOSED AMENDMENTS AND ADDITIONS HIGHLIGHTED

Section 1

Scope of application

(1) This act governs

handling addictive substances, their export, import and transit operations,

handling preparations containing addictive substances, preparations containing an addictive substance and a specified category 1 substance under directly applicable European Union law on drug precursors 1) (hereinafter "specified category 1 substance") and pharmaceutical drugs containing the specified category 1 substances, their export, import and transit operations and

growing poppy, cannabis and coca and export, import and liquidation of poppy straw,

growing, processing and possession of cannabis and cannabis products in small quantities for one's own needs.

Section 2

Definitions

For the purpose hereof,

addictive substances mean narcotic substances and psychotropic substances listed in Appendixes 1 to 7 to the Government Decree on the List of Addictive Substances,

preparation means a solution or a mixture of any physical condition containing an addictive substance or an addictive substance and a specified category 1 substance or a pharmaceutical drug under the Act on Pharmaceuticals containing a specified category 1 substance,

poppy straw means all above-ground parts (apart from the seeds) of poppy (Papaver somniferum L.), as well as the detritus after harvest, with the exception of whole poppy seed plants including the pods designated for decorative purposes,

cannabis means a flowering or fruit-bearing cyme of a plant of the cannabis genus or the above-ground part of a cannabis plant, a part of which is a cyme,

coca bush means all varieties of Erythroxylon bush and coca leaf means leaves of the coca bush, with the exception of leaves from which all ecgonine, cocaine and other ecgonine alkaloids have been extracted,

2a)

export or import of addictive substances, preparations containing addictive substances and poppy straw means their physical transport from one state to another.

cannabis product means in particular cannabis dry matter, extracted oil, concentrates, extracts and other mixtures obtained from cannabis.

cannabis plant means each plant of the Cannabis genus,

growing cannabis plants means the cultivation of cannabis plants until the harvest phase when its cyme is flowering or fruit-bearing,

j) cannabis processing means a process consisting in drying, extraction and other processing of the cannabis plant as well as extracting any cannabis products,

k) possession of cannabis or cannabis products means the possession or any other disposal of cannabis or cannabis products exclusively for one's own needs. Possession of cannabis or cannabis products is not considered as the possession of addictive substances and preparations under section 3 hereof.

Section 3

Handling addictive substances and preparations

Handling addictive substances and preparations means

research, production, processing, purchasing, supplying and using addictive substances and preparations,

purchase and sale of addictive substances and preparations and acquiring and forfeiting any other rights in rem and obligations related thereto, mediation of such contracts and representation in making such contracts.

Addictive substances specified in Appendixes 3 and 4 to the Government Decree on the List of Addictive Substances and any preparations containing those substances may be used only for limited research, scientific and very limited therapeutic purposes defined in the handling permit. Other addictive substances and preparations containing such substances may only be used for therapeutic, scientific, educational, veterinary or other purposes based on a permission of the Ministry of Health, unless set down otherwise herein.

Section 15

Prohibitions

It is prohibited

to place addictive substances and preparations in customs warehouses and free zones,

to send addictive substances and preparations

by mail as ordinary consignments,

through post office boxes, or

to persons who are not authorised to handle such substances and preparations,

to pass to another person in any way any mushroom of the Psilocybe genus,

to extract opium from poppy plants (Papaver somniferum L.),

to extract cannabis resin and substances that belong to the group of tetrahydrocannabinols from the cannabis plant (Cannabis genus) cannabis products in a manner that is not permitted and under the conditions set down herein, without prejudice to the extraction of such substances for therapeutic use or scientific purposes under the conditions set out herein and in the Act on Pharmaceuticals6),

to advertise any addictive substances and preparations if the advertisement is not aimed at professional public under the act governing advertising regulation.

Section 24

Cannabis, coca and poppy

(1) It is prohibited

to grow strains and varieties of the cannabis plant (Cannabis genus) that may contain more than 0.3 % of substances that belong to the tetrahydrocannabinol group, with the exception of growing under section 24c or based on a licence granted hereunder; the prohibition does not apply to growing varieties of cannabis plants (Cannabis genus) for research purposes, for the purpose of cultivating new varieties and preserving genetic diversity by scientific and research workplaces established by operation of law or specified by the state in the handling permission,

to grow plants of Erythroxylon genus (coca bush), c) grow varieties of poppy (Papaver somniferum L.), that may contain over 0.8 % morphine in the dry matter of the pods; the prohibition does not apply to growing varieties of poppy (Papaver somniferum L.) for research and experimental purposes, for the purpose of cultivating new varieties and preserving genetic diversity by scientific and research workplaces.

Section 24c

Growing, processing and possession of cannabis for one's own needs

Only an individual over 18 years of age without a criminal record with a permanent residence in the Czech Republic is allowed to grow a cannabis plant, to process cannabis and possess cannabis or cannabis products for his/her own needs, provided that

they grow and process the maximum of five cannabis plants in the house or on land or in a building situated on land that is not publicly accessible and is used based on a private title,

they possess the maximum quantity of 1250 g of cannabis dry matter or cannabis products extracted from such a quantity in the house used based on a private title, or the maximum quantity of 30 g of cannabis dry matter or cannabis products extracted from such a quantity outside the house.

A person without a criminal record for the purpose hereof is a person who has not been convicted of an intentional offence or is treated as if they have never been convicted.

An individual who grows cannabis plants, processes and possesses cannabis or cannabis products for their own needs under subsection 1, is obliged to prevent free access to third persons to the cannabis plants, cannabis and cannabis products.

Section 39

Administrative infractions

An individual who grows poppy or cannabis on the total area exceeding 100 m2 commits an administrative infractions if

they fail to comply with the duty to notify under section 29,

they state incorrect or incomplete information in reports under section 29.

An individual commits an administrative infraction by

possessing addictive substances in a small quantity for their own needs unlawfully,

growing plants or fungi containing addictive substances for their own needs unlawfully, or

enabling a person under 18 years of age to use addictive substances unlawfully, unless it is an offence of greater gravity, or

provides a third person with access to the cannabis plants, cannabis or cannabis products in conflict with section 24c (2).

For an administrative infraction under subsection 1 paragraph a) a fine of up to CZK 100.000 may be imposed and for an administrative infraction under subsection 1 paragraph b) a fine of up to CZK 200.000 may be imposed. By an order on the spot, for an administrative infraction under subsection 1 a fine of up to CZK 5.000 may be imposed.

For an administrative infraction under subsection 2 a fine of up to CZK 15.000 may be imposed.
