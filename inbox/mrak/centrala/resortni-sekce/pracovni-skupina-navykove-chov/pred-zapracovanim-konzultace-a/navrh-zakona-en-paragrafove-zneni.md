THE TEXT OF THE PART OF THE ACT ON ADDICTIVE SUBSTANCES,  AS AMENDED,
WITH THE PROPOSED AMENDMENTS AND ADDITIONS HIGHLIGHTED
Section 1
Scope of application
(1) This act governs
a)handling addictive substances, their export, import and transit operations,
b)handling preparations containing addictive substances, preparations containing an
addictive substance and a specifed category 1 substance under directly applicable
European Union law on drug precursors 1) (hereinafter "specifed category 1
substance") and pharmaceutical drugs containing the specifed category 1
substances, their export, import and transit operations and
c)growing poppy, cannabis and coca and export, import and liquidation of poppy
straw,
d) growing, processing and possession of cannabis and cannabis products in small

quantities for one's own needs.
Section 2
Definitions
For the purpose hereof,
a) addictive substances mean narcotic substances and psychotropic substances
listed in Appendixes 1 to 7 to the Government Decree on the List of Addictive
Substances,
b) preparation means a solution or a mixture of any physical condition containing an
addictive substance or an addictive substance and a specifed category 1 substance
or a pharmaceutical drug under the Act on Pharmaceuticals containing a specifed
category 1 substance,
c) poppy straw means all above-ground parts (apart from the seeds) of poppy
(Papaver somniferum L.), as well as the detritus after harvest, with the exception of
whole poppy seed plants including the pods designated for decorative purposes,
d) cannabis means a fowering or fruit-bearing cyme of a plant of the cannabis
genus or the above-ground part of a cannabis plant, a part of which is a cyme,
e) coca bush means all varieties of Erythroxylon bush and coca leaf means leaves of
the coca bush, with the exception of leaves from which all ecgonine, cocaine and
other ecgonine alkaloids have been extracted,
2a)
f) export or import of addictive substances, preparations containing addictive
substances and poppy straw means their physical transport from one state to
another.
g)cannabis product means in particular cannabis dry matter, extracted oil,

concentrates, extracts and other mixtures obtained from cannabis.
h)cannabis plant means each plant of the Cannabis genus,

i) growing cannabis plants means the cultivation of cannabis plants until the harvest

phase when its cyme is fowering or fruit-bearing,
j) cannabis processing means a process consisting in drying, extraction and other
processing of the cannabis plant as well as extracting any cannabis products,
k) possession of cannabis or cannabis products means the possession or any other
disposal of cannabis or cannabis products exclusively for one's own needs.
1

Possession of cannabis or cannabis products is not considered as the possession of
addictive substances and preparations under section 3 hereof.
Section 3
Handling addictive substances and preparations
(1) Handling addictive substances and preparations means
a) research, production, processing, purchasing, supplying and using addictive
substances and preparations,
b) purchase and sale of addictive substances and preparations and acquiring and
forfeiting any other rights in rem and obligations related thereto, mediation of such
contracts and representation in making such contracts.
(2) Addictive substances specifed in Appendixes 3 and 4 to the Government Decree
on the List of Addictive Substances and any preparations containing those
substances may be used only for limited research, scientifc and very limited
therapeutic purposes defned in the handling permit. Other addictive substances and
preparations containing such substances may only be used for therapeutic, scientifc,
educational, veterinary or other purposes based on a permission of the Ministry of
Health, unless set down otherwise herein.
Section 15
Prohibitions
It is prohibited
a)to place addictive substances and preparations in customs warehouses and free
zones,
b) to send addictive substances and preparations
1.by mail as ordinary consignments,
2. through post ofce boxes, or
3. to persons who are not authorised to handle such substances and preparations,
c) to pass to another person in any way any mushroom of the Psilocybe genus,
d) to extract opium from poppy plants (Papaver somniferum L.),
e) to extract cannabis resin and substances that belong to the group of
tetrahydrocannabinols from the cannabis plant (Cannabis genus) cannabis
products in a manner that is not permitted and under the conditions set
down herein, without prejudice to the extraction of such substances for therapeutic
use or scientifc purposes under the conditions set out herein and in the Act on
Pharmaceuticals6),
f) to advertise any addictive substances and preparations if the advertisement is not
aimed at professional public under the act governing advertising regulation.
Section 24
Cannabis, coca and poppy
(1) It is prohibited
a) to grow strains and varieties of the cannabis plant (Cannabis genus) that may
contain more than 0.3 % of substances that belong to the tetrahydrocannabinol
group, with the exception of growing under section 24c or based on a licence
granted hereunder; the prohibition does not apply to growing varieties of cannabis
plants (Cannabis genus) for research purposes, for the purpose of cultivating new
varieties and preserving genetic diversity by scientifc and research workplaces
established by operation of law or specifed by the state in the handling permission,
2

b) to grow plants of Erythroxylon genus (coca bush), c) grow varieties of poppy
(Papaver somniferum L.), that may contain over 0.8 % morphine in the dry matter of
the pods; the prohibition does not apply to growing varieties of poppy (Papaver
somniferum L.) for research and experimental purposes, for the purpose of
cultivating new varieties and preserving genetic diversity by scientifc and research
workplaces.
Section 24c
Growing, processing and possession of cannabis for one's own needs
(1)

  Only an individual over 18 years of age without a criminal record with a

permanent residence in the Czech Republic is allowed to grow a cannabis plant, to
process cannabis and possess cannabis or cannabis products for his/her own needs,
provided that
a)
  they grow and process the maximum of fve cannabis plants in the house or on

land or in a building situated on land that is not publicly accessible and is used based
on a private title,
b)
  they possess the maximum quantity of 1250 g of cannabis dry matter or cannabis

products extracted from such a quantity in the house used based on a private title,
or the maximum quantity of 30 g of cannabis dry matter or cannabis products
extracted from such a quantity outside the house.
(2)

   A person without a criminal record for the purpose hereof is a person who has

not been convicted of an intentional ofence or is treated as if they have never been
convicted.
(3)

 An individual who grows cannabis plants, processes and possesses cannabis or

cannabis products for their own needs under subsection 1, is obliged to prevent free
access to third persons to the cannabis plants, cannabis and cannabis products.
Section 39
Administrative infractions
(1) An individual who grows poppy or cannabis on the total area exceeding 100 m2
commits an administrative infractions if
a)they fail to comply with the duty to notify under section 29,
b) they state incorrect or incomplete information in reports under section 29.
(2) An individual commits an administrative infraction by
a)possessing addictive substances in a small quantity for their own needs unlawfully,
b) growing plants or fungi containing addictive substances for their own needs
unlawfully, or
c) enabling a person under 18 years of age to use addictive substances unlawfully,
unless it is an ofence of greater gravity, or
d) provides a third person with access to the cannabis plants, cannabis or cannabis

products in confict with section 24c (2).
(3) For an administrative infraction under subsection 1 paragraph a) a fne of up to
CZK 100.000 may be imposed and for an administrative infraction under subsection
1 paragraph b) a fne of up to CZK 200.000 may be imposed. By an order on the spot,
for an administrative infraction under subsection 1 a fne of up to CZK 5.000 may be
imposed.
3

(4) For an administrative infraction under subsection 2 a fne of up to CZK 15.000
may be imposed.
