Maybe THC should just be regulated

Permission to grow a limited number of plants for personal use
Growers educated at point of sale
Seeds with balanced THC to CBD ratio could be advertised and subsidized

Commercial growing not allowed discouraging the breeding of ultra high THC strains
Growing and storage permitted in areas without access of other people
Harsh penalties for distribution to minors

## Snímek 5

Hopes and reservations

Most users grow their own cannabis because it no longer poses a risk
Risk goes down and so prices drop on the black market causing drug dealers to choose between quitting or moving to more profitable merchandise
Smaller black market and clear distinction between legal and illegal behaviour causes the prevalence of minors to go down
Police focuses on dangerous drugs and large scale operations saving taxpayer money

Some people will never grow their own cannabis  because they cannot or because they do not want to, and so black market unfortunately survives
The inability to trade cannabis for money simply leads to barter
The only factor restricting the THC percentages is the skill of the grower because a wide range of strains is readily available
