# J. Storch - psychoaktivita na bunecnych receptorech

> Převedeno z mraku (`Centrala/Resortní sekce/Pracovní skupina Návykové chování/navykove_chovani/various_docs/J. Storch - psychoaktivita na bunecnych receptorech.docx`). Neověřený návrh, čeká na kurátora.

Zdravim Vas,

nase spolecnosti jsou totiz aktivni v zadostech o schvaleni prirodniho i syntetickeho CBD jako nove potraviny (Novel food). Prihlaska na prirodni CBD byla pozastavena kvuli tomu, ze EC prirodni CBD povazovala za narkotikum. EC zmenila nazor pred dvema tydny v dusledku rozsudku Evropskeho soudniho dvora, kdy nam prihlasku na prirodni CBD vratila do Novel Food procesu s timto vyjadrenim:

In light of the comments received from applicants and of the recent Court’s judgment in case C-663/18*, the Commission has reviewed its preliminary assessment and concludes that cannabidiol should not be considered as drug within the meaning of the United Nations Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961 in so far as it does not have psychotropic effect. As a consequence, cannabidiol can be qualified as food, provided that also the other conditions of Article 2 of Regulation (EC) No178/2002 are met. We hereby inform you that the verification of the validity of your application is therefore resumed.

*Case C-663/18, Judgment of the Court of 19 November 2020, B S and C A, ECLI:EU:C:2020:938.

Kanabinol (CBN) neni v EU povazovan za narkotikum. Naopak v UK ho za narkotikum povazuji. Je pravda, ze CBN se mirne vaze na CB1 receptory, coz zpusobuje jeho velmi mirny psychotropni efekt. Pred rozsudkem ECJ vsechny prirodni kanabinoidy byly povazovany za narkotikum, jelikoz se ziskavaji z vrcholiku konopi, ale to s timto rozsudkem padlo. THC samozrejmem jako narkotikum zustava. Jak to bude s CBN do budoucna v ramci potravin, tezko rict. Kazdopadne ho nelze do potravin pridavat. Ostatne jako vsechny dalsi kanabinoidy, pokud nebudou zapsany do Union listu. Farmaceuticke vyuziti vsech kanabinoidu je ale otevrene, coz by melo byt to hlavni.
