Subject:
Draft EU statements - 62nd CND Session - international scheduling - explanation

On 7 March the Council (Justice and Home Affairs) adopted the Council Decision on the Position to be
adopted on behalf of the EU in the 62nd Session of the CND on international scheduling (6576/19).
Following the HDG meeting on 1 March 2019, the Commission services prepared the draft statements
in annex, regarding the explanation of vote on the international scheduling of cannabis and cannabis-
related substances. Although this is unsure whether these statements will be needed, the Presidency
suggests having them ready in case.
The draft statement in section 1 of the annex aims at explaining the reasons for the vote in favour of the
postponement of the vote on cannabis and cannabis-related substances. It would be expressed by the EU
if useful depending on the circumstances of the decision on postponement.
The draft statement in section 2 of the annex contains a proposal for an EU statement that could be
expressed only in the case that the vote on the scheduling of cannabis and cannabis-related substances
would still take place. The aim of this second statement is to explain why in such case, the EU Member
States abstain from the vote.

ANNEX

1. Explanation for the vote in favour of postponement
The European Union was not in a position to prepare a common positon on the cannabis-related
rescheduling proposals through an appropriate procedure ahead of the 62nd session of the
Commission on Narcotic Drugs.
This was mainly due to the late submission by the World Health Organisation of these
recommendations ahead of the Commission on Narcotic Drugs. As there was no pre-announcement
of these recommendations at the reconvened session in December, it was not possible to prepare
such a position.
The European Union is committed to an evidence-based policy-making. It was impossible to
evaluate the impact of the recommendations on the re-scheduling of cannabis and cannabis-related
substance within the short delay available before the 62nd session of the Commission on Narcotic
Drugs. Therefore, the Member States of the European Union in the Commission on Narcotic Drugs
voted for a postponement of the vote on the cannabis-related recommendations.
The vote should take place once a thorough impact assessment of the consequences of the cannabis-
related re-scheduling recommendations has been carried out by the UNODC together with the
WHO and INCB and subsequently been presented to the Commission on Narcotic Drugs.
2. Explanation relating to abstention
As explained in relation to the postponement of the vote, for which the Member States of the
European Union in the Commission on Narcotic Drugs voted in favour, it was not possible for the
European Union to prepare a common position on the cannabis-related recommendations. For this
reason, the Member States of the European Union in the Commission on Narcotic Drugs will
abstain from the vote on these recommendations.
This abstention should not be considered as a vote against the recommendations, but just as an
expression of the European Union not being ready to vote at this stage. We hope, however, that the
issue will be put on the agenda at a future meeting of the Commission on Narcotic Drugs, once the
thorough impact assessment we suggested has been carried out and presented to the Commission on
Narcotic Drugs.
