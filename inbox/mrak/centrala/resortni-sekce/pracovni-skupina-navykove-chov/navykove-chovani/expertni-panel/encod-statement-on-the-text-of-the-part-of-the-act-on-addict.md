(A) QUESTIONS FOR ALL:

These questions, from our point of view, do not require any specific expertise and almost everyone

can present a valuable view on the subject. If you are not comfortable with some of these, do not

hesitate to skip.

1

1. In the Pirates' proposal to legalize cultivation, drying and processing, and possession of

a small quantity of cannabis for personal use (hereinafter referred to as "the proposal
Pirates "), do you see any benefits for the majority of the society?

Here is a study about the decriminalisation / depenalisation in Portugal. I suppose you know that in

the 90s Portugal made a decriminalisation of the drugs. It is a sensible drug policy that does not

punish the user. If the police catch her/him with less than 10 daily dose, she/he has to go to a

interview with a social worker and such social worker just have to sign a paper of proof that you do

not have a problem of addiction. It is a good system, but it needs budget to implement.

https://kar.kent.ac.uk/13325/

Another question we need to ask is the quantity of money the state is getting for such sanctions /

fines. I think it is important question to know if the depenalization could be.

Then, almost 20% of Czech population is using cannabis. And there is usually the self-cultivation.

Taking into account that the population of Czech Republic is more than 10 million of people, the

state will stop stigmatizing, prosecuting and penalizing around 2 million of people. In 2017, there

were 5.549 offences for drugs, the 15% were by use and personal possession. You can find all this

data in the EMCDDA Report of Czech Republic 2017.

http://www.emcdda.europa.eu/emcdda-home-page_en

The regulation of cannabis around the world teach us that some kind of supplying prevents the

minors to start using cannabis. Some studies in United States talk about that. I do not know if there

is some about self-cultivation. But, the logis says that if the self-cultivation is allowed, you will

reduce the organised crime on cannabis, therefore it could prevent some minor consumption.

2. Do you see any threats to the majority of the society in the Pirates proposal?

We do not. The first reason is because it is not a big change, it is the next step. But if you preview

some threats, you can also prevent them by implementing Harm Reduction programs. Without

stigmatization to users, in a health, evidence-based and civil society approach that can be offered

to the specific population in such services.

3. Do you generally support the possibility of cannabis cultivation for the personal use?

Yes, we do. We support the self-cultivation because we think it is the most legitimate way to

acquire the substance. Each person can choose between buying a vegetable, being a member of

an association to make a collective cultivation or grow her/himself their own vegetables. We can

not compare the vegetables with the cannabis, but we want to point out, the options of the user

with all the informations we have available.

Also, the self-cultivation is low-risk regulation, you can see it in the Guide of Transform:

https://www.tdpf.org.uk/resources/publications/how-regulate-cannabis-practical-guide

about in the US too.

5. How many grams of cannabis (dry weight) should be allowed for adults to keep outside

the dwelling for personal use?

The possession could be ten daily doses, but maybe it is too much. I think, if you already have the

limit of ten grams, could be a good option, because this amount is already managed by

policy-makers and other authorities, easy to implement.

6. In addition to the number of grams of dry matter, should the number of cultivated plants

also be limited ? How many plants do you think should be an adult person allowed to grow

So, it is difficult to say for an activist, for us all that you can demonstrate that is for your personal

7. Should it be possible to donate some quantities of hemp grown to another adult?

It is something that is already happening everywhere, so, why not? But we are talking about hemp

or about cannabis?

It is this kind of topics that shows a contradiction of the prohibition. So, you can not forbid the

sharing in private place because it is crashing with the inviolability of home and it is happening

everywhere all the time, but it is forbidden in most of the legislations on drugs. We do not know

how the democratic countries can apply this prohibition that crash with the Human Rights.

1. Do you think it is possible to take care of your health with home-grown hemp? To which

extent? How do you think? Is it complicated and costly?

Yes, when the self-growers can have all the information available and the network of cannabis

culture, making seminars of growing cannabis, harm reduction and grow shops. As a person who is

growing has to know the concrete conditions the plant needs, to identificate plagues or harmful

fungi. In consequence, this dynamic is generating such cannabis culture that keeps informed

users, so more responsible users.

If you can accompany all the regulations on drugs with Harm Reduction tools, developed from

users vision, it could reduce the harms caused by the prohibition and the desinformation. For

example, you can implement an analysis service, and the growers can go there to analyze their

substance. It is also useful for the evaluation of the policy.

We think that the self-cultivation represents the freedom of election on cannabis acquirement. First,

in the Czech Republic the growing is so common, it is a social reality already existent. Normally,

the people who grow vegetables in their homes take care about which procedures they use, it is

under their responsibility. You might also publish some recommendations on growing.

2. What do you think is the risk that prevalence will increase after the adoption of the

Pirates proposal cannabis use among juveniles and the entire population?

We do not think so, the studies demonstrate that after a regulation, the consumption of minors

decrease, because it is less accessible to the minors, especially if there is a regulated system of

supplying. Also, you can implement courses to young people and special campaigns addressed to

them.

4

3. What do you think is the risk of people growing and consuming cannabis grown in

accordance with the Pirates' proposal will cause pathological phenomena related to

cannabis use?

First, we think that the grow should be not accessible from outside, to prevent minors and third

people to get it, outside the private context. So, it could be regulated by law, for example.

Second, we do not know exactly what you ask by ¨pathological phenomena¨. If you ask in the

meaning of medical pathology, the studies demonstrate that it is not true, you can ask to some

doctor and request a second opinion. If you ask in the meaning of social pathology, we think it is a

construction of the prohibitionist speech.

4. In your opinion, accepting the Pirates' proposal would lead to curb the Black Market with

cannabis?

We think, you can reduce a bit the black market, but just the people who have place to grow, not

everybody can. For that you can not abolish the black market if there is not regulation on cannabis

supplying. Even then, if the prices of the substance are high, also you will keep a percentage of

black market, for that are important the social and communitary models of cannabis supplying, with

fair prices.

5. What health risks do you think will emerge after to the adoption of the proposal?

We think that if it is good formulated and implemented, accompanied by harm reduction policies

and analysis service, it would be inclusively beneficial for the society. Because it is already

happening in the Czech Republic, it is a social reality, so you will improve the access and reduce

the legal harms for the users, so it just can be well being for the society.

6. What do you think the impact of adopting the Pirates' proposal on the environment would

be?

We think that all the depenalization:

-
Reduces harms caused by the prosecution of users avoiding some harms caused by the

prohibitionist policies. Including the costs related to that, administration, police, etc costs.
-
Increasement of the availability of the information related to the substance.
-
Decreasement of the anti police conduct among the users.
-
Also, it can reduce the robberies of crops. And the possibility to denounce it in the police

office. In consequence it will increase the social security for the growers and the society.

7. How likely it is, do you think, in the case of outdoor cultivation, that heavy metals,

pesticides or other dangerous substances, extracted from the soil by the plant, will enter

the user's body?

5

So, there are rules of organic and biological growing by the European Union. Also, you can

analyse the ground or you can grow in pots. So, to that you just have to implement the correct

rules, the self-cultivation also needs a regulation. We do not expect that a Parliament would accept

free self-cultivation. It should be accompanied by rules and recommendations.
