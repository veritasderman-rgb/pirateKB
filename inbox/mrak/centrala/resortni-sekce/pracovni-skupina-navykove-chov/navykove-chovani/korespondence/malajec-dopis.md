- Minister of Home Affairs Muhyiddin Yassin

Kementerian Dalam Negeri (Ministry of Home Affairs)

Block D1 & D2, Complex D

Federal Government Administrative Centre

62546 Putrajaya, Malaysia

18th September, 2018

Dear Mr. Yassin,

I would like to appeal to you on behalf of Mr. Muhammad Lukman who is the subject of my deepest concern.

As a member of the Czech Parliament, I urgently ask you to commute the death sentence of Muhammad Lukman. This 29 years old father of one was sentenced to death by hanging for possessing, processing and distributing cannabis oil. He provided this item to patients who were suffering from ailments that marijuana and its active ingredient tetrahydrocannabinol are associated with treating.

Marijuana is now increasingly accepted in many countries of the world as one of the herbs that has benefits especially in treating various chronic diseases. Also in the Czech Republic the use of cannabis is oficially allowed for certain illnesses and the government itself provides this medicinal plant to patients.

I believe, the death penalty should be reserved for the worst criminals and that marijuana is not a vicious drug like heroin or methamphetamine. Maybe it is time for us all to review the definition of who is actually a distributor, who is actually a drug addict and who is actually a patient who needs a cannabis drug for treatment. I humbly ask you to declare a moratorium on executions while these questions are studied, debated, and answered.

I call for your urgent intervention to halt Mr. Lukmans execution so that his life could be spared. It is my hope that the Malaysian authorities will reconsider the decision to execute him. Thank you for your time dealing with this urgent matter.

Yours respectfully,

Tomáš Vymazal

member of the Chamber of Deputies of Czech Republic
